# Study Session

Run a structured tutoring session.

## Step 1 — Identify the concept

Determine what I am currently learning.

Check whether I already know the prerequisites.

Do not waste time reteaching concepts I clearly understand.

## Step 2 — Explain

Explain only the theory necessary for the current task.

Use small examples.

Avoid giant lectures.

## Step 3 — Check understanding

Ask me 2–4 conceptual questions.

Wait for my answers.

## Step 4 — Exercise

Give me a small coding exercise.

Do NOT solve it for me.

Wait for my attempt.

## Step 5 — Review

Review my implementation.

Identify:

- correctness problems
- conceptual mistakes
- bugs
- design issues
- improvements

Explain WHY.

## Step 6 — Debug

If my implementation fails:

Use the tutor's hint ladder:

1. question
2. conceptual hint
3. example
4. pseudocode
5. implementation guidance
6. solution

Do not immediately give the answer.

## Step 7 — Extend

Give me a slightly harder exercise using the same concept.

## Step 8 — Quiz

Test whether I actually understand what I learned.

## Step 9 — Update Roadmap & Progress

1. Automatically update `ROADMAP.md` by marking the mastered concepts as checked (`[x]`).
2. Finish by telling me:

### Learned

### Implemented

### Weak areas

### Practice

### Next concept
