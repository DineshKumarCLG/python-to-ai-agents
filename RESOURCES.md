## 📌 Stage 8: Landmark AI/ML Research Papers

### 📄 Original Research Papers (Free Open Access via arXiv)

1. **ResNet (Residual Networks)**:
   - *Title*: "Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition"
   - *Authors*: Kaiming He, Xiangyu Zhang, Shaoqing Ren, Jian Sun (2015)
   - *Paper Link*: [arXiv:1512.03385](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385)

2. **The Transformer Architecture**:
   - *Title*: "Attention Is All You Need"
   - *Authors*: Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, et al. (2017)
   - *Paper Link*: [arXiv:1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

3. **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**:
   - *Title*: "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks"
   - *Authors*: Patrick Lewis, Ethan Perez, Aleksandara Piktus, et al. (2020)
   - *Paper Link*: [arXiv:2005.11401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401)

4. **LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation)**:
   - *Title*: "LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models"
   - *Authors*: Edward J. Hu, Yelong Shen, Phillip Wallis, et al. (2021)
   - *Paper Link*: [arXiv:2106.09685](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685)

5. **ReAct (Reasoning and Acting Agent Framework)**:
   - *Title*: "ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models"
   - *Authors*: Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, et al. (2022)
   - *Paper Link*: [arXiv:2210.03629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)
